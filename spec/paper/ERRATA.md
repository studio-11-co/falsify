# Errata: PRML v0.1 preprint (`prml-v0.1-preprint.tex`)

The preprint is kept as published. Corrections are listed here instead of editing the published text.

## 2026-10-17: evidence wording (section on what PRML establishes)

The preprint says PRML establishes that a criteria object "existed no later than a stated, independently anchored time and has not changed since". The second half is broader than what a timestamp shows.

Corrected reading: a validated anchor supports checking that a disclosed copy matches the version committed no later than the anchor time. It does not show that no other version was created or used in the meantime, when testing began, or whether that version was followed.

The same correction was made to the specification as an editorial erratum (PRML-v0.1.md, Change Log, 2026-10-17). No normative rule, canonical byte or digest changes.
